Privacy Policy — EK Network Tools

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This Privacy Policy applies to EKCast and EK Drop.

1. Network Transmission & Local Sharing

EK Drop: This app transfers files directly between devices on your local Wi-Fi network. Files are not routed through or stored on our servers.

EKCast: This app streams podcasts from public third-party servers. Your IP address may be visible to those podcast hosts as part of standard internet routing, but we do not track your listening history.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal information or access your files. We do not store your file transfers or podcast subscriptions on external servers.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID) to serve ads. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

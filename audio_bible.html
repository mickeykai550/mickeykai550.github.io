Privacy Policy — Audio Bible Apps

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This Privacy Policy describes how our Audio Bible applications ("we", "us") handle your information. This policy applies to all our Bible audio apps published under the package name com.ek.kbible*.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any personal files. Our apps do not require account registration.

2. Audio Playback & Streaming

Our apps provide audio playback of Bible passages. Audio files may be bundled locally within the app or streamed from our servers/third-party content delivery networks (CDNs). When streaming, your device's IP address and basic connection data are processed by the CDN solely to deliver the audio. We do not track your listening habits or link them to your identity.

3. Playback History & Bookmarks

Any playback history, bookmarks, or favorites you save are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or have access to this data. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in system settings or through the app's built-in settings.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Permissions

Our apps may request the following permissions:

  • Internet: to stream audio and load advertisements.
  • Foreground Service / Wake Lock: to continue audio playback when the screen is off or the app is in the background.
  • Storage (Optional): if you choose to download audio files for offline listening, files are saved to your device's app-specific storage and are not accessed by us.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.

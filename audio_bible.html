Privacy Policy — Audio Bible Apps

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This Privacy Policy describes how our Audio Bible applications ("we", "us") handle your information. This policy applies to all our Bible audio apps published under the package name com.ek.kbible*.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any personal files. Our apps do not require account registration.

2. Audio Playback

All audio files and text content in our apps are bundled locally within the application. We do not stream audio from external servers, and we do not record or transmit any audio through your device's microphone.

3. Playback History & Bookmarks

Any playback history, bookmarks, or favorites you save are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or have access to this data. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in system settings or through the app's built-in settings.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Permissions

Our apps may request the following permissions:

Our apps may request the following permissions to function properly:

  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Required to load advertisements and check network connectivity.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Required to keep the device awake so audio playback can continue when the screen is off or the app is running in the background.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to read application expansion files (OBB) and to save your playback progress, bookmarks, and app settings locally on your device. We do not access, collect, or modify your personal photos, videos, or unrelated documents.
  • Google Play License (CHECK_LICENSE): Used to verify app installation validity.

Note: We do not use these permissions to collect, store, or transmit your personal identity, location, or private files.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.

Privacy Policy — EK Audio Tools

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This Privacy Policy applies to EK Audio Mark and EK Readify.

1. Microphone & Audio Data

For recording apps (EK Audio Mark), audio is recorded locally on your device. We do not upload your voice recordings to any servers. You have full control over your recorded files.

For TTS apps (EK Readify), text is processed locally using your device's Text-to-Speech engine.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal information, contacts, or files. We do not have access to the content you record or listen to.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID) to serve ads. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

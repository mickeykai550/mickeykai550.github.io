Privacy Policy for Fake Go

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Fake Go ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information in connection with your use of our mobile application, Fake Go (Package Name: com.ek.fakego).

1. Information We Collect and Process

Fake Go is designed to allow users to simulate mock location provider parameters on their devices. We process data strictly to deliver this core functionality:

  • Location Data: The app processes mock location settings and simulated geographical coordinates entered or selected by you. This location data is processed locally on your device to execute the location spoofing feature and is not stored, transmitted, or collected on external servers by us.
  • Device and Usage Information: We do not directly collect personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect technical device identifiers for ad serving and operational performance.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support the free availability of the application, Fake Go uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for ad analytics and performance tracking.

Specifically, we integrate:

  • Google AdMob: AdMob uses advertising identifiers (such as AAID) and non-personal diagnostic data to display personalized or non-personalized advertisements. You can review Google's privacy policies at Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions Required

To function properly, the app requests specific system permissions on Android:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required to interact with the device's location provider frameworks.
  • ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION: Required to set simulated location attributes when developer options / mock location settings are enabled by the user.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch map tiles, search locations, and load advertisements.

4. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not collect or store your personal location history or user profile data on external databases, we do not retain your private data. Any map preferences or temporary settings remain strictly on your local device storage.

5. Children's Privacy

Fake Go does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.

Privacy Policy — EKZipFlow

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This Privacy Policy describes how EKZipFlow ("we", "us") handles your information when you use our application.

1. File Content

EKZipFlow does not collect, upload, transmit, or store the contents of any archive files you open or extract using the Application. All extraction is performed locally on your device. We have no access to your files.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any personal files. EKZipFlow does not require account registration.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Permissions

EKZipFlow uses the Storage Access Framework (SAF) to let you choose where to extract files. We do not request broad storage permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
